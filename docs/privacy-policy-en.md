# RURI MINER MONITOR Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

RURI MINER MONITOR is a monitoring app that uses public mining-pool APIs or publicly accessible web lookup functions. It does not require a developer-operated account or backend server; core settings and monitoring data are stored on the device.

## Information processed
- Public wallet addresses or miner/mining-account identifiers entered by the user, selected coins/pools, aliases, and general settings
- Optional read-only API tokens for certain official APIs such as F2Pool (encrypted with Android Keystore and excluded from normal backups)
- Hashrate, worker count, accepted/rejected shares, API status and short error summaries (up to 7 days)
- An app-specific Support ID for device-bound offline reward coupons

The App does not request private keys, seed/recovery phrases, wallet passwords, or withdrawal credentials.

## Network and third-party services
The App may connect directly to official/public HTTPS APIs or web pages of registered pools. Those services may process ordinary connection information such as IP address and request metadata. The App may also access a public GitHub repository for signed pool/API configuration and app-update information.

## Advertising and consent
The App may use Google AdMob and Google UMP. Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information defined by Google for ad delivery, fraud prevention, measurement and diagnostics. Where consent is required, UMP is used to collect and manage advertising privacy choices.

## Backups and exports
User-initiated JSON backups may include registered pools, public wallet/miner addresses, and some general settings. Personal custom-adapter definitions, secret API tokens, advertising/reward entitlements, and status history are excluded from normal backups. Monitoring history may be exported separately as CSV.

## Notifications
Android notification permission may be requested for lower-hashrate alerts. Denying permission does not block other App functions, but warning notifications may not be shown.

## Reports and support
Reports and support actions open the user's email app and are not sent automatically. Do not send passwords, private keys, seed phrases, withdrawal credentials, or unrestricted API secrets.

## Retention and deletion
- Status history: up to 7 days
- Registered pools/wallet addresses/general settings: until deleted by the user or App data is cleared
- Read-only API tokens: until deleted by the user or App data is cleared; stored using Android Keystore
